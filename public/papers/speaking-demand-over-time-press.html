Wellspoken Institute · Research news · November 24, 2026

Speaking ratings moved in both directions across long-standing occupations

A comparison of two federal occupational databases found that speaking-importance ratings rose in 64 occupations, fell in 96 and stayed the same in 63. The median change was zero.

The Institute studied 223 occupations that retained the same code, title and description and received a new analyst rating between O*NET Database releases 15.0 and 31.0. Recent ratings were collected in different years. New and redefined occupations are outside this comparison.
